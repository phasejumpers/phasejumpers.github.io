**Till Death We Do Art** is [Ninia Serpen's]({% link _posts/characters/2026-11-11-NINIA.md %}) personal shuttle. It is well known as acting as a both a runabout and an early rescue unit due to its ability to cope with heavy gravitational fields.

The shuttle itself is a modified Jemison II from LIM, modified significantly to fit its owner. It contains a small kitchenette, bedroom, cryopod in case of emergency, autocannon and rocket systems, as well as a countermeasure system. Its owner typically carries a small hoverbike in the rear. 

It was last seen being hauled back by a Triourocore employee after Serpen abandoned the craft at Axothan.
