Triourocore is a conglomerate faction formed of 3 main companies, Serpentine Heavy Industries, Synx Mining and Salvage, and Rescue Wolf Rangers, as well as hundreds of subcompanies. A union of sorts, Triourocore provides operational overhead, payment processing, coms arrays, custom tooling, and so on. In exchange, Triourocore collects a small amount of dues, and acts in the interests of workers rights.

Triourocore started as a solo company, Serpentine Heavy Industries, started by commander N. Serpen, as a way for combat-modified veterans like herself to find work in a system prejeduced against altered humans. As her system was flooded with gangs, mobs, mafias, and other organized crime, she made a union with two other companies, Synx Mining and Salvage, and Rescue Wolf Rangers, forming Triourocore, with each company having an equal share. 

As Triourocore expanded, their political power in the system did as well. This led to heavy conflicts with local gangs and larger mobs, resulting in Triourocore having a larger than usual security force, similar to some local militias. This has caused them to come into conflict with larger governments as a lobbying party for workers rights, presiding over large protests as a deterrant against policing actions.

Triourocore is well regarded in the richer sectors, however their "humanity first" policy has lead to accusations of overreach and overpolicing in less funded sectors. 

Serpen herself is a controversal figure, with praise toward her policy and efficent worksmanship, but is also known for being abraisive and absolutist in talks with other factions, leading to an unstable position in the public sphere.
