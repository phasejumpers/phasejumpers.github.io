Widely considered top of the line within the jurisdictions of CASA.

Strictly speaking, Ad Astra Mercenaries are supposed to be freelance and independent; however, the current ongoing contract between AAM and CASA includes an exclusivity clause as required by the Military Technology Export Restriction (MeTER) laws in CASA. Based on the current CASA annual budget bill, most analysts find it next to impossible for anyone to be capable of or willing to buy out AAM's contract and offer enough to violate MeTER.

AAM first arrived in the Engima system as the escort for the colony ship "Flight of the Dammed," but following the catastrophic accident and destruction of the colony ship, the contingency clause in AAM's contract with the Company came into effect and locked AAM into acting as a peacekeeping force till the establishment of a permanent colonial presence, without additional pay.

With the establishment of Homecoming by TRI and Granum by PTF, AAM announced the completion of its contract with the Company and was released from further obligations and privileges.

Due to the already somewhat close working relationship between TRI and AAM during the Trelan conflict, AAM continued working with TRI following the end of the escort contract and eventually became one of the founding factions of CASA.

AAM designs often focus on performance and only performance, sometimes to the point where that razor-edge tuned performance starts getting in the way of practicality and utility.
