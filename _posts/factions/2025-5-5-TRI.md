Triourocore is a conglomerate company owned by [Ninia Serpen]({% link _posts/characters/2026-11-11-NINIA.md %}) alongside two co-owners, Aonnya Sinnrasse (deceased, 6/15/3311) and Ninia's wife, who's name has been lost to history (deceased, 2/17/3312).

Triourocore started as three individual companies, Serpentine Heavy Industries, owned by Serpen, Lamia Logistics, owned by Sinnrasse, and Synx Mining and Salvage, owned by her wife, in the Betelgeuse system. 

A system of harsh climates and even harsher working conditions, Triourocore formed when the three companies combined security forces, merging under one parent, due to agressive piracy in the region and lack of support from the government in the system. Betelgeuse had very relaxed worker protections, and was rich in extraction resources, leading to low wages for hard work, also leading to many workers feeling disenfranchised and afraid to enter. Combine that with the high costs of living, and SHI's already existing union/co-op, and TRI ended up forming the largest unionized workforce in the system. 

This unionized workforce was able to exude heavy economic and political sway, either bleeding other corporations out of business or absorbing them, as well as bending the local government, eventually merging it into itself. Depending on who you were, Triourocore either acted as a robinhood of the common worker, ensuring safe working conditions and good pay, or a mob that was more than willing to use violence to get their way. Attempts were made to restrict Triourocore's fleet, including the infamous Sabo-tabby unit, but they either failed or were ignored. 

Triourocore itself acts as a facilitator and logistical backbone for many companies, governments, and other units, providing a little bit of everything. Shipping, manufacturing, construction, banking, agreculture, salvage, mining, medical support, and other essentials for a system to function. While often not the best company for the job at any one thing, they tended to be second best at everything, able to adapt and overcome with preexisting designs and a scrappy habbit of bodging things together. This unfortunately means that Triourocore is lacking in its own designs, and is generally very inefficient--a lumbering bureaucracy, if a well meaning one.

In the current system, Triourocore has largely been absorbed into CASA, providing most of their civil services, though they stil serve on the open market.
