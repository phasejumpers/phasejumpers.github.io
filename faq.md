## How does Combat Work?

Combat on the server is what we like to describe as vanilla plus, much like the rest of the server. All weapons are vanilla, just with subtle tweaks and changes to make them fit their roles better.

The main one you as a player will notice are the ranges. You can now lock targets, including via AI blocks, from 7.5km out. Railguns and Torpedos can nail targets from up to 7km away, artillery and missiles from 6km, assault from 5.5km. Rockets launch from 5km and can limitedly track targets, autocannons hit at 4, and all other weapons, including gatlings, hit from 3.5km. 

All guns have also had their max automatic engagement range upped from 800m to 3km, or 2km for the gatling turret. On small grid, instead it is 1600m, with no adjustment for the gatling gun specifically. This means that if a target is close, your guns will automatically track and engage, no lock required.

Rockets, Torpedos, and Missiles can track targets effectively across large distances, but do have a limited turning radius, with missiles being the most manuverable, rockets the least, and torpedos being in between. 

Finally, gatlings have been adjusted to have an actually reasonable fire rate. All standard gatling turrets fire at 2500 rounds per minute
