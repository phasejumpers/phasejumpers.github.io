Lets start with the basics. First, some history.

A large group of different companies, factions, and military forces were gathered by The Company under one overarching contract to colonize a system some 22,000 lightyears away from Sol. Who is The Company? Doesn't really matter now. What does matter is that the colony ship these factions were on was destroyed due to a hydrogen explosion. While some of the larger factions managed to get some smaller ships off, the majority were scattered throughout the system in escape pods. Out of the 500,000 sent on the trip, only around 2,000 survived. With no other choice than to start building where they were, colonization efforts began, what was discovered to be the 5th attempt at colonizing this system.

A coalition formed between a small group of factions, AAM, TRI, and AEG, called CASA. This soon became the government of the Serpentis sector, named after its primary colonizer, Ninia Serpen. A similar governance formed later, from MUN, LIM, PTF, and MCI, known as LUNA, which presided over the Tarsonis sector as the system's first free government. CASA would transition to a democracy later in its existence. These two governments acted as the main superpowers, occasionally warring, but mostly peaceful, acting to manage the two sectors. CASA had the higher population and richer in GDP, with strong worker protections, while LUNA had the greater exports in IP and the sciences, along with greater cultural freedom. These two giants are still around today, thirty years after their creation.

Finally, there are the phase gates. These massive megastructures themselves count as megafauna, a mixture of technology and flesh turned into transport methods between the deep black between sectors. How they operate is not very well understood, but it is believed to be a form of gravity catapult, accelerating structures in such a way to bypass the jump drive mass ratio problem and safely decelerate structures on the other end. This allows the travel of distances on the order of multiple thousands of astronomical units, expanding trade and making rapid reaction a possibility. For a while, the gates themselves were believed to be spreading a pathogen, however this is now understood to be a part of the gates natural reproductive cycle, and can be mitigated with a vaccine alongside regular feeding of materials into the gates themselves.

Recently, a new set of phase gates were discovered, much older, more mature, with a much greater travel distance. These gates, dubbed wing gates, were quickly snapped up by both CASA and LUNA for exploration, launching probes into what is now recognized as the outer rings of the system. These gates were then opened to a small number of general colonists to prepare infrastructure for a large population migrating later. 



So who are you, in this boundless universe? 

You are likely part of the second generation--the children of the 5th wave, thirty years in the future, now having grown into full adults. Out in the deep, you have been sent into a new part of the system to explore and colonize for your respective faction, preparing for the aforementioned population migration. You may have splintered off from your parents faction, you may be part of an old one, you may be something completely new. Regardless, your primary objective is to explore, create, and document your findings for more members of your faction to join in from the old world.
